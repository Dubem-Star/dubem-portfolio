About Me

I'm Chidubem, a recent Computer Information Systems graduate and passionate web developer in training. I specialize in building clean, user-friendly websites and interactive web apps. My journey began with a Udemy web development course and an internship at an electricity company, where I gained valuable real-world tech exposure.

So far, I’ve built a To-Do List App with drag-and-drop support and localStorage, and a Quiz App with multi-page navigation, answer tracking, and score calculation. When I’m not coding, you’ll find me sketching out new app ideas or exploring the latest UI/UX design trends.
